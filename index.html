PeakForm

A shared home for a coach and their athletes' training.

PeakForm lets a coach build training plans, review completed activities, and track an athlete's recovery — sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, readiness — in one place, with the athlete controlling exactly what the coach can see.

PeakForm is currently in active development and testing — not yet publicly launched. This page exists to describe the project during that phase, including to third-party services (such as Strava and intervals.icu) we're integrating with.

Training plans

Seasons, goals, races and weekly training built by the coach, followed by the athlete.

Activities & wellness

Manual entry, file import, or synced from Strava / intervals.icu (and, through it, Garmin, Oura, WHOOP and more).

Consent-based sharing

An athlete grants a coach access permission by permission — never more than they choose.
