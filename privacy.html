About this document

PeakForm is an application for coaches and athletes — it lets a coach build training plans, track completed activities, wellness data (sleep, recovery, heart rate) and communication between coach and athlete in one place. This document explains what personal data we process in connection with using PeakForm, why, and what rights you have.

2

What data we process

Depending on how you use PeakForm (as an athlete or a coach), we process:

Account & profile

Name, e-mail, role (athlete/coach), password (stored only as a hash, never in readable form).

Training data

Training plans, planned workouts, races and goals, personal records.

Completed activities

Manually entered or synced activities — distance, time, heart rate, power, pace, calories.

Wellness data

Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, readiness/stress scores, morning and evening check-ins.

Nutrition & hydration

Food and fluid intake entries, if an athlete logs them.

Communication

Comments on workouts between coach and athlete, in-app notifications.

3

Where the data comes from

Data can enter PeakForm three ways:

  • Manual entry — an athlete or coach types it directly into the app.
  • File import — e.g. a spreadsheet export (CSV) that you upload yourself.
  • Connecting a third-party account — if you choose to connect your Strava or intervals.icu account, PeakForm downloads your activities and wellness data from that account after your explicit consent (an OAuth sign-in). If you have additional devices connected within your own intervals.icu account (e.g. Garmin, Polar, Suunto, Coros, Oura, WHOOP), PeakForm receives that data too — only what you've authorized, never more.

You can disconnect a third-party account at any time in the app's settings — we immediately delete the stored access credentials for that account.

4

Who can see your data

Your data is visible to you, and — only if you've explicitly allowed it — to a coach you have an established relationship with in the app. A coach's access is always split into specific permissions you grant individually (e.g. view training plan, view completed activities, view wellness data, view food and hydration, view health flags). A coach never sees more than the permissions you've granted, and no other athlete or unapproved third party can see your data at all.

5

How data is secured

  • Passwords are stored only as a hash, never in readable form.
  • Access and refresh tokens for connected accounts (Strava, intervals.icu) are stored encrypted.
  • Signing in to the app uses short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens — if a refresh token is reused (a sign of leakage), the whole session is invalidated.
  • All communication between your browser and the app is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data is stored in a database that no one outside the app's operation can access.
6

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request an export of all your data at any time in settings, or request account deletion — in which case we permanently remove your personal data, except where we're legally required to retain it longer. When you disconnect Strava or intervals.icu, we immediately delete the stored access credentials for that account.

7

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data or share it with advertising or analytics third parties. The only external services PeakForm works with are the ones you connect yourself (Strava, intervals.icu) — they operate under their own terms of use and privacy policies, which we recommend reviewing before connecting an account.

8

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy (data export),
  • correct inaccurate data,
  • erasure ("the right to be forgotten"),
  • data portability to another provider,
  • withdraw consent for a connected third-party account at any time,
  • lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.
9

Changes to this policy

Because PeakForm is currently under active development, this policy may change as the app's features grow. We'll notify users in-app of material changes. The date at the top of this document reflects the last update.
